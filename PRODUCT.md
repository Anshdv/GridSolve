# GridSolve

## Register
Brand (marketing + tool). Public pages communicate; the explorer is the product surface.

## Users & purpose
Data-center and generation developers deciding where interconnection is actually deliverable. They need to screen substations (Power) and later diligence sites (Risk, Combined, compare, export).

## Personality
Instrument-grade, dark, precise. Engineering grid, Archivo + Martian Mono, electric blue accent on near-black. Not playful SaaS, not cream editorial.

## Anti-references
Generic AI landing pages, Inter/Space Grotesk stacks, nested card galleries, rainbow dashboards.

## Principles
- Same visual system on marketing and app chrome
- Numbers over adjectives
- Estimates are planning tools, not engineering advice
- Access is decided server-side
- Sample is the instrument. Screening ($3,000/mo) is a live hunt. Diligence ($30,000/yr) is the write-down: Risk, compare, print, CSV. Output is internal screening — not a utility study.
